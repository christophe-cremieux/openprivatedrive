About Storage Cleanup

The cleanup job performs the following actions:

  • Removes **Expired Upload Sessions** (older than 24 hours) and their chunk data.
  • Deletes **Orphaned Temporary Decrypted Files** (older than 1 hour) left behind during interrupted downloads.
  • Note: Thumbnails and Previews are currently managed via the permanent delete flow, but automated orphan scanning is planned for a future update.
